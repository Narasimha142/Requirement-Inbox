Requirement Inbox

A Chrome extension that pulls job requirements from Gmail, Dice, job boards and startup job posts, filters them to your profile, lets you reply to many at once with your resume attached, and tracks replies and applications.

Download ZIP Setup guide View on GitHub

What it does

  • Reads requirement emails from Gmail and job-alert emails from Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor and Monster
  • Pulls jobs from Dice and Hacker News "Who is hiring" posts
  • Filters out junk and roles that don't match your profile
  • Sends bulk replies with your resume attached and tracks follow-ups
  • Runs entirely in your browser with your own Google sign-in, so there's no server

Install

  1. Download the ZIP above and unzip it somewhere you'll keep it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
  4. Follow the setup guide to connect Gmail.
